The family standard

The name on the wall
is the family on the job.

ANJ Construction has been family-run since 2015. We keep the crew tight, the lines straight, and our word good — on a homeowner's fireplace and on a four-story commercial facade alike.

Plumb, level, square

Checked with the level and the line, not the eye. If a course drifts, it comes down and gets relaid.

Clean joints

Mortar struck consistently and washed down. On repairs, we match the mortar color so the patch disappears.

A tidy site

We protect everything around the wall, and the job isn't finished until the site is broom-clean.
