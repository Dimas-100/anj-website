FAQ

Common masonry questions.

Answers about estimates, service areas, residential and commercial masonry, and repair work.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Share the project location, photos if available, and the general scope so ANJ can review the next step.

Do you handle residential and commercial masonry?

Yes. ANJ works on residential masonry, commercial masonry, repairs, chimneys, patios, facades, additions, brick, block and stone.

What areas do you serve?

ANJ serves Georgia communities including Winder, Lawrenceville, Metro Atlanta, Savannah and surrounding areas.

Can you repair existing masonry?

Yes. Repair work can include repointing, mortar matching, brick replacement, chimney repair and restoration depending on site conditions.

What information should I send before an estimate?

Helpful details include the property address or city, project type, photos, preferred timeline and any known concerns such as cracking, drainage or access.
