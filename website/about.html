How we work

Masonry is only as good as the preparation under it.

Every ANJ project starts with site conditions: access, drainage, existing masonry, tie-ins, flashing prep and the way water will move after the work is complete. That planning keeps the finished wall, chimney, patio or facade looking clean and performing correctly.

We keep crews focused and accountable. Layout, material matching, mortar joints, cleanup and final detailing are handled with the same standard whether the project is a small chimney repair or a larger commercial block package.
